Privacy Policy

PocoPals does not require an account and does not collect personal information. Your progress, including the animals you have collected, your terrarium layout, coins and settings, is stored only on your device.

Advertising: PocoPals shows ads provided by Google AdMob, including banner ads, full-screen ads and optional rewarded ads. AdMob may collect and use device identifiers (such as the advertising ID) and usage data to deliver and measure ads. Where required, we ask for your consent through Google's consent form. You can change your choices any time from Settings > Privacy settings. For details, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Purchases: Any in-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details.

Children: PocoPals is not directed to children.

Changes: We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.

Contact: rikichin.games@gmail.com

プライバシーポリシー

PocoPals はアカウント登録不要で、個人情報を収集しません。集めたアニマル、テラリウムの配置、コイン、設定などの進行状況は、お使いの端末内にのみ保存されます。

子ども向けではありません: 本アプリは子どもを対象としたものではありません。

改定: 本ポリシーは改定されることがあります。最新版の日付は上記のとおりです。
